About Us

Father and Son Trades was started by Jeremiah Kinikin, who has spent years working as both a mobile mechanic and a home handyman. He's turned wrenches in driveways and fixed what needed fixing in living rooms — whatever the job called for.

Jeremiah built this business on three things: showing up when he says he will, telling customers the truth about what a job actually needs, and charging a fair price for it. No inflated estimates, no unnecessary work.

His hope is simple — to build something that lasts. A business his family can be proud of, and one day, hand down.
